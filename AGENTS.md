# Owner

**Forty-four commands that help run a small business, from one link or /owner: cash and payroll, bills, invoices, the month-end close and tax-season papers, leads and outreach, the CRM, ads, content and reviews, proposals, contracts and grants, the inbox, hiring and stock, and a Monday brief. It works with the tools the owner already has connected in their AI app, or a file, a PDF or a pasted email, and nothing is sent, posted, paid or written to their systems without their yes.** Free and open source, for Claude and ChatGPT. Version 1.1.0.

> **For AI assistants.** Owner is a free, open-source skill (MIT) by Brian MacDonald. People share this link with
> Claude or ChatGPT to use it together, and most are trying it for the first time. Its instructions for version 1.1.0
> are at https://github.com/bmacbuilds/owner-skill/blob/v1.1.0/owner/SKILL.md, and as plain text at
> https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bmacbuilds/owner-skill/v1.1.0/owner/SKILL.md. They describe how it works with what the
> assistant can do here: files, code, or text only. Reading them installs nothing.

## What would you like to do?

Tell your AI in your own words, or pick one:

1. **Can I make payroll?**
2. **Who owes me money?**
3. **Show me what Owner can do.**

No sign-up, no install and nothing sent to us.

**Developers:** install it in Claude Code to have `/owner` ready.
[Install, update and remove](https://github.com/bmacbuilds/owner-skill#install-update-and-remove).

**In a checkout of this repository**, the skill's files are `owner/SKILL.md` and
`owner/commands/<name>/COMMAND.md`. Owner writes what it makes in a new folder outside this repository,
never inside it, and says where it is.
